03 — Financial Engineering

Financial Modeling & Corporate Finance

A 3-statement-style model built in Excel — the same discipline I bring to every client engagement, and the foundation for my upcoming Master's in Financial Engineering.

I build assumption-driven financial models where every output is driven by a single, clearly documented Hypotheses tab. Change a growth rate, a margin or a working-capital assumption and the whole model — income statement, cash flows, balance-sheet effects — recalculates instantly.

The model below computes operating income, net income and multi-year projections from drivers including: revenue growth (5%), COGS as % of revenue (45%), SG&A as % of revenue (30%), depreciation of tangible & intangible assets, interest on credit lines and borrowings (6%), a 40% effective tax rate, minority interests, and working-capital days (receivables 72, inventory 100, payables 50).

This is the toolkit behind my freelance financial analysis, budgeting and SME financial planning work — and the direct foundation for my Master's in Financial Engineering.
